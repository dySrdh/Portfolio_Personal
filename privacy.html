Privacy Policy — AutoClipper

Last updated: 9 October 2026

AutoClipper is a personal automation tool operated by Doddy Surya Dharma. It turns recordings of live streams into short videos and uploads them to YouTube channels owned and managed by the operator. It is not offered to the public.

What data the app accesses

Through the YouTube Data API, the app accesses only the YouTube account that the operator signs in with. It uses this access to upload videos, set their titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails and publish schedule, and to update the title and description of the operator's own live broadcasts.

How data is stored

OAuth tokens and processing logs are stored only on the operator's own computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. No data from other YouTube users is collected or stored.

Google and YouTube

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google Account permissions page. Deleting the local token file also removes the app's access from the operator's computer.
